Rehearse on the patient's own anatomy.

Veridien is building a modular abdominal simulator: organ and pathology modules you swap per procedure, including modules made from a patient's own MRI or CT, in PVA hydrogel tuned to behave like real tissue. We're looking for surgeons to help shape it.

Stay in touch

The idea

Every rehearsal should match the patient on the table.

Most simulators use one generic anatomy for everyone. We fabricate phantoms from a patient's own imaging, in a hydrogel we tune to match how real tissue cuts, holds a suture, and handles, so a surgeon can work through the case before operating.
