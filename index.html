One simulator.
Every procedure.

Most trainers are built for one task and one anatomy, then used for everyone. We're designing a single base that takes different organ modules, pathology variants, and, when a case calls for it, a module made from the patient's own MRI or CT, so one simulator can follow training from procedure to procedure.

From the case to the rehearsal, on one base.

  1. Choose

    Pick the organ modules a procedure involves: hepatobiliary, upper GI, bowel, or pelvic.

  2. Customize

    Swap in a pathology variant, or a module segmented from the patient's own scans, all in PVA (polyvinyl alcohol) hydrogel tuned to handle like real tissue.

  3. Rehearse

    Dock the modules into the base under the laparoscopic cover, then cut, suture, and navigate before ever touching the patient.
